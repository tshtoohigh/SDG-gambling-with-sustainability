Reading the tags

What each tag actually promises

The tag is a grading decision made by a human at the bench, not a marketing label. Here's the standard each one has to meet.

Vintage Rare

Datable to before 2000

Verified from construction, label typography, care-tag format and country of manufacture — not from vibes. If we can't date it confidently we grade it down to Statement Piece instead. 20 kinds in the catalogue; typically resells $45–180.

Designer Label

A recognised label, authenticated

Heritage or contemporary, checked before packing, in good condition. 30 kinds in the catalogue; typically resells $35–120.

Statement Piece

Unlabelled but unforgettable

Bold print, unusual cut, or a texture you won't find on a high-street rail. This is also where anything we couldn't authenticate lands. 45 kinds; typically $20–60.

Seasonal Pick

Chosen for the month you order

Linen in June, brushed flannel in November. Good condition, weather-appropriate, immediately wearable. 45 kinds; typically $15–40.

Everyday Staple

The backbone of every box — and the biggest category on purpose

60 kinds, more than any other tag, because this is most of what we rescue and most of what actually gets worn. Solid basics with real life left in them that were headed for a bale: merino crew-necks, straight-leg denim, unpilled cotton tees. Three or four of these are in every box, guaranteed, with no roll involved. Typically resells $8–25.
