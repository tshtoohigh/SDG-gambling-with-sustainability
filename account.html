My vault

Your boxes, your pieces, your credit

Boxes you buy arrive here sealed. Open them when you're ready — the draw happens at that moment, against the published odds — then keep what you want, have it delivered, or sell it back to us for store credit.

The loop

Shop, open, keep or sell back

1. Buy a box

It lands in your vault sealed. Nothing is drawn yet.

2. Open it

The feature slots roll against the published odds. This is the real draw, and it happens once.

3. It's yours

Everything lands in your vault with its grade and tag.

4a. Deliver it

Ships Tuesdays and Fridays. Recycled kraft, paper tape, zero plastic.

4b. Or sell it back

Store credit, and the piece goes back into the pool for someone else.

Two things we want to be straight about

Store credit is not cash. It can only be spent on boxes here. It is never withdrawable and never transferable. That's deliberate: a cash-out is precisely what would turn this into gambling, so there isn't one.

Selling back is always worse value than keeping the piece. We pay well under typical resale because anything returned has to be re-graded, re-photographed and re-shipped. Sell-back exists so nothing gets binned — not as a way to make money, and we won't dress it up as one.
