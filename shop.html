What "standout per box" actually means

It's the chance that at least one of your feature slots lands on something other than an Everyday Staple. For the Classic Box that's 1 − (0.35 × 0.35) = 87.8%, because it has two independent feature slots. The Starter Box has one slot, so it's simply 45%. We show the arithmetic because a single big percentage with no working is how these sites usually mislead people.
