Odds & fairness

What exactly are the odds?

Each box has one or two feature slots. Every feature slot is rolled independently against the table below. The remaining pieces are guaranteed everyday staples, not lottery tickets.

Does spinning the wheel decide what I get?

No, and we want to be blunt about it. The wheel on this site is a visualiser. It shows you the published probability distribution, drawn to scale, so you can see what you're buying a chance at. Spinning it is free, unlimited, reserves nothing and changes nothing.

Your actual box is assembled by a human sorter pulling against the same ratios. We publish the realised monthly rates on our Impact page so you can verify the packing matches the advertising.

How do I know the wheel isn't rigged to look better than the real odds?

Three things, all checkable:

  • Segment sizes are generated from the odds table. A 2% outcome occupies 2% of the circle. We don't inflate rare slices to make them feel reachable, which is the standard trick.
  • The outcome is drawn before the animation starts. Nothing about the spin's length or speed influences it, and there's no near-miss weighting that parks the pointer just past a rare slice.
  • The wheel keeps a running tally. Spin twenty times and it shows your observed rate next to the published rate. Small samples wander, but the numbers converge.

The code is in the open too: it's a single file, assets/js/wheel.js, and the odds table it reads is in assets/js/site-data.js.

Are the odds per box or per item?

Per feature slot. This matters. A Classic Box has two feature slots, so your chance of at least one landing on something better than a basic is 1 − (0.35 × 0.35) = 87.8%, not 65%. A Starter Box has one slot, so it's simply 45%.

We show that arithmetic on the shop page because quoting a single flattering percentage with no working is the most common way these sites mislead people.

Can you change the odds after I order?

No. The odds table is versioned and dated. An order is fulfilled against the table that was published when you paid, even if we revise it the next day. Current version: 1 September 2026.

Is this gambling?

It's a randomised product bundle, and we take the comparison seriously rather than brushing it off. Two structural differences matter: every outcome has real use value (the worst case is still five wearable garments in your size), and there's no cash-out, no resale market we operate, and no way to chase losses with us.

We also deliberately omit the mechanics that make loot boxes compulsive: no streak bonuses, no countdown timers, no "one slot left" pressure, and subscriptions capped at one box per month. If you want to stop, there's nothing to unwind.

We do sell one paid second chance, Trade Up, and it is deliberately built so it isn't a re-roll: it can only ever improve your outcome, and it's capped at one per feature slot. There's no version of it where you pay and end up worse off, which is the thing that makes re-rolls worth chasing.

Trade Up

What is Trade Up?

If a feature slot lands on an Everyday Staple or a Seasonal Pick, you can pay 10% of the box price to give that piece back and draw again from a pool with it — and everything below it — removed.

That removal is the whole point: because everything at or below your result is taken out, the new draw cannot be worse than what you gave up. It's a guaranteed upgrade, not a gamble.

The fee is $1.00 on Starter, $2.20 on Classic, $4.50 on Premium. The piece you trade back isn't destroyed — it's re-graded into the pool for someone else's box.

Isn't that just a paid re-roll?

No, and the difference is the reason we were willing to build it. A re-roll can land you somewhere worse, which is exactly what makes re-rolls worth chasing — you pay again to undo a loss you just paid for. Trade Up has no downside outcome, so there is nothing to chase.

Three deliberate limits keep it that way:

  • One per feature slot. Hard cap. No escalating prices, no "one more go". The most you can ever add to a box is 10% per slot.
  • Only from the two disappointing outcomes. You can't trade up from a good result to farm a better one.
  • Vintage Rare is never a Trade Up outcome. It's the scarcest thing we stock, and it's what the Premium tier's price is built on. Selling a $2.20 shortcut to it would be dishonest to Premium buyers and would drain the stock their boxes are promised. Vintage Rare is only ever won on your original odds.

When do I decide?

After your box arrives and you've handled the actual garment — not from a screen before it ships. You open the box, and if a feature piece isn't for you, Trade Up is offered on your order page for 30 days alongside the free swap.

We did it that way round on purpose. Deciding while looking at a real garment is a different act from deciding while watching an animation, and only one of those is a considered choice. The wheel on this site remains a simulator: you can spin it and preview Trade Up for free, forever, without spending anything.

What's the difference between Trade Up and a free swap?

A free swap (included on Classic and Premium) is for fit — you get the same kind of piece in your other size, at no cost. Use it when something doesn't fit.

Trade Up is for taste, and it's paid — you're buying a step up the rarity ladder. Use it when something fits fine but bores you. They're independent: using one doesn't consume the other.

Sourcing & quality

Where does the clothing actually come from?

From the by-the-pound overflow stream at 24 partner thrift stores. Charity and thrift shops receive far more than they can hang — limited rail space, limited staff hours — so the surplus is baled and sold on by weight, typically for export or shredding. We buy those bales.

This is why a $10 box can contain something genuinely good: the garment was never individually priced in the first place. Nobody undercut a charity shop to get it to you.

Is anything in the box new?

Never. No new garments, no deadstock, no factory overruns, no "sustainable basics" padding. Occasionally a rescued piece still has its original tags attached, which we note on the card — but it came to us secondhand like everything else.

What condition should I expect?

Every piece is graded by hand before packing:

  • Excellent — indistinguishable from unworn.
  • Very Good — light wear, no visible flaws at arm's length.
  • Good — honest wear: slight fading or softening, structurally sound.

Nothing with holes, stains, broken zips or heavy pilling goes into a box — it goes to textile recycling. Roughly a third of what we buy fails grading, and we'd rather tell you that than imply a perfect yield. Premium boxes are Very Good or better throughout.

Is it cleaned before it ships?

Yes. Everything is laundered on a cold cycle with eco detergent, line-dried where the fabric allows, then steamed and wrapped in paper. Wool and delicates are handled separately. It arrives ready to wear, not ready to wash.

How do you verify a "designer" or "vintage" piece?

Labels are checked against reference archives for construction details, label typography, care-tag format and country of manufacture. Vintage Rare means we can date the garment pre-2000 from those markers. Anything we can't confidently authenticate is graded down to Statement Piece rather than given the benefit of the doubt — the incentive runs the right way.

Sizing & fit

How do you handle sizing if I can't see the items?

You choose a primary size and one alternate at checkout. Giving us two roughly doubles the pool our sorters can pull from, which measurably improves what lands in your box.

We grade by measurement, not label, because vintage sizing is wildly inconsistent — a 1970s size 12 is nothing like a modern one. Chest, waist and inseam are measured flat and matched to your selection.

Do you cover plus sizes and petite?

We stock XS–3XL. Being straight with you: the donation stream genuinely thins out above 2XL and in petite lengths, especially for vintage. If we can't fill a feature slot properly in your size, we tell you before shipping and offer a swap to a different tier or a refund — we don't quietly substitute something that won't fit.

Can I specify styles I don't want?

Yes, and please do. There's a free-text exclusions field at checkout: fabrics you react to, styles you'll never wear, colours you hate, or anything you'd rather not receive for personal reasons. Exclusions are honoured absolutely. They narrow the pool, but they never change your published odds.

Shipping

When will my box arrive?

Boxes are packed and dispatched on Tuesdays and Fridays. From dispatch: Starter 3–5 business days, Classic 2–4, Premium 1–3. Shipping is free on Classic and Premium; Starter is $4 ground.

We only offer ground shipping. Air freight would undercut the entire point of the business, so we don't sell an express option at any price.

What does it arrive in?

A recycled kraft box, paper tape, paper tissue wrap and a printed note card listing every garment, its grade and the shop it came from. Zero plastic — no poly mailers, no bags, no bubble wrap. The box is reusable and the card is compostable.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently the US, Canada and the UK. We won't expand further until we have a sorting bench on the destination continent — flying secondhand clothes across an ocean to prove an environmental point would be absurd.

Can I subscribe?

Yes, capped at one box per month, cancellable in one click with no retention flow and no "are you sure?" maze. We cap it deliberately: a service that talks about textile waste shouldn't be encouraging you to accumulate clothing faster than you can wear it.

Returns & swaps for blind items

What's the return policy on something I couldn't see first?

Split into three clear cases, because "blind" shouldn't mean "no recourse":

  • It doesn't fit. Free swap. Classic includes one, Premium includes two with a prepaid label. We send a replacement in your alternate size and re-circulate the original.
  • We got it wrong. Wrong size band, a flaw we should have caught, or a guarantee we didn't honour — full refund or replacement, your choice, and you keep the box. That's on us, not you.
  • The roll didn't go your way. No refund, and we want to be upfront rather than bury it. You bought a published chance and we honoured it. Refunding unlucky-but-correct outcomes would mean charging lucky customers more to cover it.
How long do I have?

30 days from delivery for swaps and for anything we got wrong. Unworn, tags on where they came with tags. Start it from your order page, or just email us.

What happens to something I send back?

It's re-graded and goes back into the pool for another box. Nothing returned is destroyed or landfilled, and returns don't count twice toward our diverted-weight counter — we deduct them and re-add on the second shipment.

Can I return the whole box unopened?

Yes, within 14 days, unopened and with the seal intact, for a full refund minus return postage. Once it's open we move to the swap process instead, since we can no longer re-grade the contents as a sealed unit.

Something arrived damaged or unwearable.

Send us a photo and we'll replace the piece or refund its share of the box price, whichever you prefer — no need to ship it back. That's a grading failure on our side and it's logged against the sorter's bench so we can fix the cause.

Didn't answer it?

Email hello@secondspin.example and a human replies within one working day. If your question should have been on this page, we'll add it and credit you.
